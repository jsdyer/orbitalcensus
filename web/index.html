ABOUT THE CENSUS

A catalogue, not a live sky.

These are CelesTrak SATCAT records captured for comparison, not real-time locations or collision predictions. Launch dates describe the launch associated with an object; debris may be catalogued much later. Counts describe this catalogue and do not include every object in space.

Reading the evidence

“Active” follows CelesTrak operational status codes +, P, B, S and X for payloads. It does not necessarily mean transmitting. “Decay recorded” uses a reported decay date or status D. Owner codes can represent countries, organizations or joint programmes.

Default comparisons track identity and status fields. Check “Include orbital measurements” to also compare period, inclination, apogee, perigee and radar cross section. Changed measurements alone do not establish a manoeuvre.

The collector checks daily and downloads the full CSV only when its source edition or configured coverage changes. Fourteen captures are retained by default. Source edition, capture time and last check are distinct; schedules can be delayed.
